3. Watchlist & 5-Point Entry Setup Engine QUANTITATIVE CRITERIA SPECIFICATION

The Watchlist & Entry Radar evaluates candidate stocks through a strict 5-point quantitative gate. Candidates passing all criteria earn the STRONG ENTRY status, highlighting prime risk/reward setups.

1
Multi-Source Ratings Consensus Score ≥ 65 / 100
Synthesizes Seeking Alpha Quant (algorithmic factor model), Wall Street Consensus (sell-side analyst targets), Zacks Investment Research (earnings momentum), and Investing.com into a normalized 0–100 scale. Ensures rigorous multi-firm validation.
2
RSI Momentum Buy-Zone 38 ≤ RSI ≤ 58
Filters out dangerous overbought extremes (RSI > 70) while targeting candidates consolidating or reversing out of oversold territory with accelerating positive momentum.
3
Moving Average Trend Alignment Price > SMA50 > SMA200
Demands confirmed structural alignment: Market Price must trade above an upward-sloping 50-day Simple Moving Average, which in turn must remain above the institutional 200-day trendline.
4
Volume Accumulation Confirmation Relative Volume ≥ 1.0×
Confirms that price movement is backed by real institutional liquidity. Trading volume must match or exceed the trailing 20-day average volume to validate institutional accumulation.
5
Consensus Target Upside Potential Upside ≥ +15.0%
Calculates margin of safety based on mean analyst price targets. Guarantees that the expected reward meaningfully justifies the capital at risk before opening a position.
4. Signal Center: 0–100 Scoring Engine Ranking Algorithm
Technical Score (0–100)

Calculated using 4 quantitative sub-components:

  • RSI Momentum (25 pts)
  • Trend Alignment (30 pts)
  • MACD Crossover (25 pts)
  • Volume Breakout (20 pts)
Portfolio Fit Adjuster

Dynamic multiplier based on active portfolio risk:

  • Concentration Penalty: -15 pts if position > 18% or sector > 30%.
  • Diversification Bonus: +5 pts for underrepresented high-alpha sectors.
Action Status Pills

Clear trade directives derived from composite score:

STRONG ENTRY ≥ 75 & 5/5 setup
APPROACHING 60–74 or 3–4/5
NEUTRAL / TRIM < 60 or overbought

5. Strategy Backtesting & Alert Infrastructure EXECUTION & VERIFICATION
5. 5-Point Strategy Backtester Historical Simulation
Simulation Parameters
  • Entry Threshold (Default: 75): Minimum composite score required to open a simulated trade.
  • Take-Profit Target (Default: +15%): Closes trade when price hits target gain.
  • Stop-Loss Guard (Default: -5%): Cuts position if trade moves against thesis.
  • Time-Stop Barrier (Default: 20 days): Exits position if neither target nor stop is triggered within 20 trading sessions.
  • Lookback Windows: Selectable across 6mo, 1y, 2y, or 3y daily historical data.
Performance Analytics & Audit
  • Benchmark Comparison: Direct alpha and return comparison against SPY (S&P 500 ETF).
  • Risk Metrics: Displays Sharpe Ratio, Win Rate %, Profit Factor, and Maximum Drawdown.
  • Equity Curve Chart: Visualizes strategy account trajectory vs. buy-and-hold benchmark.
  • Trade Ledger: Full audit log detailing Entry Date, Exit Date, Realized Return %, and Exit Reason (TARGET, STOP_LOSS, TIME_STOP).
6. Armed Triggers & Telegram Push Notifications Continuous Monitoring
Trigger Types & Management
  • Price Level Alerts: Trigger when price moves ABOVE resistance or BELOW dip-buy entry target.
  • RSI Alerts: Trigger on momentum reversals (e.g. RSI crossing below 40 or above 70).
  • Risk Drawdown Alerts: Automated alerts for portfolio-wide drawdown or sector breaches.
  • Armed State: Background worker evaluates all active triggers every market cycle.
Telegram Bot Integration
  • Real-Time Delivery: Instant mobile notifications when any trigger fires.
  • Actionable Alerts: Messages include ticker, trigger price, current RSI, and setup grade.
  • Zero Polling: Runs autonomously on the background worker server.
  • Configuration: Set Bot Token and Chat ID in Settings • Alerts tab.
Institutional Daily Operating Playbook
Step 1 — Morning Scan: Open Watchlist & Entry Radar to review candidate stocks scoring 4/5 or 5/5 on the criteria gate.
Step 2 — Empirical Validation: Click 🧪 Backtest on prime candidates to confirm positive alpha and win rate in recent market regimes.
Step 3 — Risk Check: Open Analytics & Risk to verify that adding the position will not breach the 35% sector cap or spike portfolio Beta.
Step 4 — Order Dispatch / Arm Trigger: Click 🔔 Arm Trigger to notify when entry price is reached, or execute directly via Scale / Rebalance.
